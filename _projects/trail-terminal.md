## what it is

A redesign of oschei.com as a faux-terminal session. Click commands or type
them; both work. Five canonical pages each addressable as URL and command.

## what it taught

How much of "design taste" turns out to be discipline about what to *leave
out* — and how the simplest framing (a shell session, with a few real
commands) carries more identity than any amount of styling.

## stack notes

Hand-rolled. Jekyll on GitHub Pages. JetBrains Mono throughout, Tokyo Night
palette. Vanilla JS. No frameworks.
